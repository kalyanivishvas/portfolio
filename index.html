How I Think

My Design Process

Even for concept work, I follow a structured process rooted in real UX methodology.

Step 01
🔍
Discover
Understand the users, the problem space, and what currently exists. Research is the foundation of every good design decision.
Step 02
🗺
Define
Synthesise findings into clear problem statements, user personas, and journey maps. Know exactly what you're solving before touching UI.
Step 03
✏
Design
From rough sketches to wireframes to polished prototypes. Iterate quickly, test ideas, and let feedback shape the final solution.
Step 04
✅
Validate
Test with peers or users. Does it solve the problem? Is it intuitive? Validate assumptions and document what could be improved next.
